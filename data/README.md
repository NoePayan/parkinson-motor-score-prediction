# Data

The data come from the **bobathon-esilv** Kaggle competition and are not redistributed here.

Download `X_train.csv`, `y_train.csv` and `X_test.csv` from the competition's Data tab and place them in this folder.

The tables are synthetic: they reproduce the structure, relationships and missingness of multi-cohort Parkinson's disease records, without any real patient data.
